Maintenance Tips for Water Damage Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Curing time for paint, floor adhesive and caulk should pass before the room sees water or hard use.
  • Future repairs match better when you have held on to extra flooring, tile and the paint information.
  • Once a year, renew the caulk at sinks, showers and tubs to keep water from entering the wall.
  • Before a contractor closes a floor or wall, request the final moisture numbers.
  • When kitchens, bathrooms or laundry rooms are rebuilt, choose backer board and moisture resistant drywall.
  • Fix the source first, and only then patch or paint a water stain.

When to Call for Water Damage Repair in New York City

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Where water ran down a wall, the paint has blistered, peeled or turned brown.
  • A leak overhead left the ceiling drywall cracked, sagging or falling apart.
  • A door frame or slab swelled, so the door rubs or fails to close.
  • The lower part of cabinet sides or bases feels soft, looks swollen or is peeling.
  • Trim around doors and along the floor is bowed and will not lie flat.
  • Tiles sound hollow, have come loose or show cracked grout since water got beneath them.
  • Wall cavities remain open where drywall was removed for drying.

Water Damage Repair, Step by Step

We follow a proven process to ensure every job is completed to the highest standards. Here's what you can expect when you choose New York City Restoration:

Verify Dryness First
